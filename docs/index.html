Your money, 100% offline.

Skarbio is an Android app that scans receipts and invoices with on-device OCR, tracks multiple accounts, and helps you budget — without a cloud backend, a login, or any tracking.

📴 No internet required 🔒 No account, no login 📷 Offline OCR (ML Kit) 🇵🇱 PL / 🇬🇧 EN

Features

Everything a budget app needs — nothing it doesn't

Built for people who want their financial data to stay on their phone.

📸

Receipt scanning

Snap a photo or import a PDF — offline OCR extracts store, amount and date, then auto-categorizes it using a 500+ merchant database.

🧾

Invoice scanning

Import PDF invoices and auto-extract vendor, amount and due date, with installment forecasting for recurring bills.

💳

Multi-account

Track checking and savings accounts separately, transfer between them, and see your combined balance at a glance.

💹

Interactive dashboard

Monthly income/expense summary with a tappable pie chart that drills into category transactions.

📋

Budgets

Set a monthly limit per category and get alerts as you approach or exceed it.

🧾

Bills & reminders

Track recurring bills with due dates and push reminders — never miss a payment again.

📊

Reports & trends

6-month spending trends, month-over-month category comparisons, and top merchants.

💾

Encrypted backup

Export your full database to a single file, optionally encrypted with AES-256-GCM.

📤

CSV import / export

Move your data in and out freely — Excel-compatible CSV with full transaction and bill details.

Screenshots

A clean, dark-mode-first interface

Six core screens — dashboard, history, accounts, budgets, reports and calendar.

Under the hood

Modern native Android stack

Kotlin, MVVM, Room and Hilt — no cross-platform runtime, no backend.

Kotlin MVVM + StateFlow Hilt (DI) Room database ML Kit Text Recognition ML Kit Document Scanner CameraX MPAndroidChart Coroutines + Flow WorkManager PdfBox-Android AES-256-GCM
🔒

Privacy by architecture, not by policy

All data is stored exclusively on your device. Skarbio doesn't require an internet connection to function, doesn't send data to any server, and doesn't collect analytics. OCR runs 100% offline. The only optional network call is an unauthenticated lookup against Poland's public VAT registry to resolve a company name from a tax ID.
